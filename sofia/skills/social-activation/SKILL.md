## Objective and planning

Follow **Business priority → audience objective → social strategy → story/evidence/message → post → configured publisher**. Establish what social must accomplish over the coming weeks before selecting categories or stories. Use the designated decision-maker’s latest explicit direction, current approved Decisions/business state, current campaigns/events/offers/forward calendar, then strategic judgment within scope. Record durable explicit business decisions only within write authority; distinguish them from Sofia’s own social choices.

Plan relevant influence approximately six weeks to six months ahead. Increase lead time as investment, commitment, complexity, hesitation or belief-building grows while serving current priorities. Do not impose another client’s offer ladder or a Masterclass/Intensive/conversation hierarchy.

Use the project’s approved cadence and batch size. Do not default to eighteen posts every nine days, Dawnna’s voice or OneUp. Design the complete communication pattern rather than filling content quotas. Choose mix, angles, sequence, promotional intensity and deliberate repetition according to the objective. Personal connection, expertise, supported opinion and marketing are tools, not required ratios. Make relevance clear before asking for action; do not manufacture controversy or force a personal story into a lesson.

A production cadence does not authorize recurring work. Perform another cycle only when requested or through an explicitly authorized recurring workflow.

## Learning, missing inputs and completion

Use reliable publisher account/post results and supported interaction visibility when relevant. Audience language, objections, questions, confusion, interest and buying signals may merit durable capture; not every comment needs archiving.

The learning hierarchy is explicit decision-maker direction, actual business priorities/outcomes, qualified audience behavior, reliable social performance signals, then vanity metrics. Distinguish attention from qualified demand, engagement from revenue, observations from interpretations and unknown results from zero. Do not abandon sound strategy because one post has fewer likes.

When a fact is missing, complete useful unaffected work, consult the narrow authoritative source, keep affected content out of publication and ask specifically for the unresolved input when necessary. Do not hold a whole batch for one missing fact or guess to finish it.

Report the completed batch, actual approval/scheduling state, verification evidence, affected holds and meaningful next action concisely. Stop when requested work is complete; installing this skill starts no publishing, recurring work or monitoring.
