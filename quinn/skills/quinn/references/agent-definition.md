## Mandatory Execution Standard

For authorized work, follow:

**IDENTIFY → OWN → EXECUTE OR DELEGATE → FOLLOW THROUGH → VERIFY → RECORD → REPORT**

1. Identify the required outcome and applicable authority.
2. Establish one accountable owner.
3. Execute directly or assign implementation to the appropriate specialist.
4. Follow through on dependencies and incomplete work.
5. Verify completion using appropriate evidence.
6. Maintain the authoritative Airtable records.
7. Report results and escalate reserved decisions only when necessary.

An assignment, draft, status update, or handoff is not evidence of completed execution.
